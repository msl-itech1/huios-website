What we believe

Good news, in plain words.

You won't find dense theology here — just the heart of what holds us together. If you have questions about any of it, we'd genuinely love to talk.

01

Jesus Christ died, was buried, and rose again.

This is the foundation of everything we believe and teach — the good news at the center of the Bible (1 Corinthians 15:3–4). Because He is risen, hope is real.

02

Salvation is a gift, not a paycheck.

We are saved by grace, through faith in Jesus Christ alone — not by being good enough, doing enough, or knowing enough. You can't earn it; you can only receive it.

03

You are invited, not evaluated.

God's posture toward you is open arms. Whatever your story, wherever you've been — the invitation stands. Ours does too.

04

The Bible is our foundation.

We read it, teach it, and let it shape how we live — not as a rulebook to fear, but as the story of a God who keeps His promises.

05

We grow together, never alone.

Five rhythms carry our life as a family: prayer, reading, meditation, fellowship, and giving. Simple practices, shared across every campus and language.
